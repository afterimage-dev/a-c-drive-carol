A retelling of the greatest ghost story in English

A C-Drive Carol

The miser is you, and the hoard is your hard drive. Three spirits will visit your desktop, and show you what you've kept.

What it looks at

Only your own , and folders. Nothing else on the drive.

Nothing leaves this computer

No network, no AI services, no analytics. Every line the spirits say is a written template filled in here.

Nothing is deleted

Files you lay to rest are moved into the Crypt (). Resurrect them any time. Only you can bury them, by emptying the Crypt yourself.

Nothing moves without you

The spirits only queue your choices. Nothing moves until you confirm a final review. Press Esc at any moment to end a visit with nothing changed.

Sample files are a few tiny stand-ins (under 40 KB in all) made just for the demo. Their sizes and dates are simulated, and labelled so.
